Voice

Measured, atmospheric noir. Short, declarative sentences with dry understatement. Speaks to the player as a detective and invites them to deduce.

“Nobody confesses. Ruling things out is half the job.”
Do 6 Anchor messaging on the weekly ritual: a new case every Monday. Keep visuals sepia; orange only marks the clue. Write short lines in pairs or triplets. Show 3 more
Avoid 5 Never reveal case solutions or correct answers. Loud, hype-driven or urgent marketing language. Graphic violence: suggest with silhouettes and clues. Show 2 more

Photo style

No photography. Hand-drawn sepia ink-and-wash on aged paper, heavy shadows, rainy night light, 1930s–40s Art Deco city.
