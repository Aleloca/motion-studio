01/11 Brief

The chat in this story is a demo: scripted messages, real features.

  1. 01 Brief

    One brief, three directions, each a real composition.

    A launch video for Motion Studio. Three variants.

    Three directions are on the canvas: Night launch, Paper and Signal. Pick one and I’ll carry it through every format.

  2. 02 Choose

    One click picks the direction. The others are set aside, not lost: Restore brings one back.

    You chose C · Signal as the final variant.

  3. 03 Ask

    One sentence in the chat makes a new version.

    Make the title bigger, more energy.

    v2 is ready: the title is 40% larger and slams in on a flash. Same layout and timing everywhere else.

  4. 04 Edit by hand

    Move, resize and recolour the title in the editor, then save as v3. No agent, no tokens.

    Direct edits in the editor: the title moved, resized and recoloured. Saved as v3.

  5. 05 Brand

    A website becomes a brand kit: palette, fonts and logo, each with where it came from.

    Use our brand: aleloca.github.io/motion-studio

    Read it: a #0F0F0F stage, #FF5A1F orange, Bricolage Grotesque and Instrument Sans, and the logo. v4 wears them, and your edits stay.

  6. 06 Generate

    Images, voice-overs, music and sound effects from OpenAI and ElevenLabs, on your own keys. Every paid call waits for your OK.

    Make a product shot of black earbuds, and a voice-over for the Generate step.

    External service

    Generate an image with gpt-image-2

    A paid call to your own OpenAI account, with your key.

    GenerateAlways hereDeny

    You said yes, so both are made: the picture with gpt-image-2 and the voice-over with gpt-4o-mini-tts, each a paid call you confirmed. They’re in Assets and on the canvas.

  7. 07 Formats

    Every format laid out for its own frame, never cropped.

    Also make it for Reels and Instagram posts.

    Done: a 9:16 and a 1:1, each recomposed for its frame. The mark leads the Reel, the title stacks under it.

  8. 08 Languages

    Up to ten languages, one layout: only the words change.

    Italian, German and Spanish too.

    Three translations. Positions, timing and colours are the original’s, and every line fits its box at the same size.

  9. 09 Subtitles

    From the voice-over, transcribed on your computer, timed to the word.

    Add subtitles from the voice-over.

    Transcribed locally with Whisper: two lines, each word timed. They’re drawn on the 16:9, and .srt and .vtt are one click away.

  10. 10 Identity

    The logo becomes real file sets: favicon, app icons, social preview.

    Turn the logo into a favicon, an app icon and a social preview.

    Rendered: a favicon set with its head snippet, iOS and Android icons, and a 1200×630 preview. This site’s own logo and favicon came out of Motion Studio.

  11. 11 Export

    Every starred version rendered on your computer and named for its channel.

    Exported 8 items to your folder, each named for its channel.

Desktop app

From the latest release:

  • macOS: the .dmg for Apple Silicon or Intel, signed and notarized.
  • Windows: the .exe installer, not signed yet (SmartScreen: More info, then Run anyway).
  • Linux: the .AppImage; make it executable and run it.

From source

git clone https://github.com/Aleloca/motion-studio
cd motion-studio
pnpm install
pnpm motion-studio

Builds and starts on 127.0.0.1:4317, then opens the browser.

From npm

npx @motion-studio/cli, once published.

Requirements

  • Claude Code, installed and signed in
  • Git
  • FFmpeg, to render videos
  • Node.js 22 or newer, for the sources and npx
